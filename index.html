Interpretation

Calculating results from the tract data…

Measure

Definition and timing

The measure is the model-based crude prevalence of women ages 50–74 who reported having a mammogram within the previous two years. The PLACES 2025 release carries this measure forward from the 2022 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System.

Geography

Coverage

The analysis includes 408 census tracts in Santa Clara County. Tract geometry comes from the Census Bureau’s TIGERweb Generalized ACS 2023 Census Tracts 500K layer. Data and boundaries join on the 11-character tract GEOID.

Limitations

How to read the results

PLACES values are modeled small-area estimates based partly on self-reported survey responses, not direct tract-level screening counts. Confidence intervals show uncertainty. Tract comparisons are descriptive, not individual behavior or causal effects.
